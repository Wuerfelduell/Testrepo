# Story: Rabenfels, the city beneath the city

Source: the owner's story concept, 13-10-2026, written in German in the project chat
(inspired by the "City of Silver" from Lord of the Mysteries). This file is a faithful English
summary for everyone working on the game. The owner decides everything; open points are at
the end (RULE 7).

---

## Premise

300 years ago, a catastrophe cut the original city of **Rabenfels** off from the world. The
city sank deep underground. An **ancient entity** awoke and bound itself to the city's
foundations. Since then an **invisible border** surrounds the **Underground City**.

- Nobody born inside the border can leave. The closer they get to the exit, the worse it
  gets: unease, nosebleeds, disorientation, voices. Those who keep going die.
- The border recognises who belongs to it, and nobody knows why. Even the player cannot bring
  arbitrary people down from above.
- **The player can cross it**, because he was born outside. He is the first person in
  centuries who can travel between both worlds. That makes him enormously valuable.
- Why exactly he can cross is a concrete cause he discovers later, tied to the catastrophe
  300 years ago. He is **not** a chosen one at the start: just a person who happens to be able
  to do something nobody else can. What he makes of it is his choice.

## The Underground City

The inhabitants are not primitive. They have books about the outside world and know stories of
forests, oceans, mountains and the sky, but nobody has seen the sun for 300 years.
About 3,000-5,000 people live there, in a working but harsh society: political intrigue,
fights over food and power. Some want to reach the outside at any cost, some believe the border
protects them from something worse, and some would do anything to stop the player from
leaving again.

## The entity and the dying city

The entity may be the reason the inhabitants are still alive: during the catastrophe it created
the protective border. Outside the border is something that nearly destroyed the people back
then, and the entity has held it back for 300 years. **Its power is fading**: monsters appear
in the outer districts more often, the artificial light flickers, whole streets become
uninhabitable. The people begin to understand that their city is slowly dying. They cannot
leave their only home, and if they stay they will eventually die.

## The central mystery

The inhabitants know the outside world exists, but they believe Rabenfels was **completely
destroyed** 300 years ago. They do not know that a huge new city, the **upper city**, has
grown right above them, with thousands of people living normal lives. Learning this is a
massive shock.

The central question: **Do the rulers of the upper city know that thousands of people still
live beneath them, and do they keep them trapped on purpose?**

## The player's role

At first nobody trusts him: a stranger who claims to come from above, possibly a spy or a fraud.
Only when he actually brings something from the outside does his standing change. Then
everyone wants something from him:
- the **Council** wants him to investigate the cause of the sealing,
- the **traders** want trade with the outside,
- the **Wardens** need materials and weapons they cannot make,
- the **Church** believes he is part of an old prophecy,
- **ordinary people** ask for tiny things that are out of their reach.

## Moments that define the tone

- **The girl and the flower:** a twelve-year-old girl asks: "Is it true that clouds sometimes
  look like animals?" Later she asks him to bring her something from outside. Not a magic
  weapon, not an artefact: a flower. When he brings it, she cries. She has never seen a real
  plant.
- **The boy who wants to be a knight:** a quest line about a boy from the Underground City who
  dreams of becoming a knight. The player trains with him, they fight creatures in the Lost
  Districts, and he grows into an important companion. Then the player learns the boy is
  seriously ill. There is a cure in the upper city; the player gets it, but it does not help.
  The illness is caused by the border. The only way to save him would be to take him out, and
  that is impossible. Breaking the border becomes personal.
- The relationship between upper and Underground City is the core of the **second half** of
  the game.

## The most important rule: not everyone can be saved

Freeing the Underground City is the central long-term goal, but it is hard and has
consequences. The border cannot simply be destroyed with a magic item; the player must first
find out why it exists. Maybe the upper city sacrificed the lower one on purpose. Maybe the
entity truly is its protector. Maybe destroying the border would release something that could
destroy both cities. Maybe there is a way to free the people without giving up the protection
entirely.

### Endings
- **A - Liberation:** the player destroys the border. The inhabitants step outside for the
  first time in centuries, but something that was imprisoned beneath Rabenfels is released too.
- **B - The sacrifice:** the player stabilises the border. The Underground City survives, but
  its people remain trapped.
- **C - The new covenant:** the player finds out what the entity really is, negotiates with it
  and finds a way to free the people step by step, at a high personal price.

This fits the project rules: wrong decisions are punished (RULE 4), and no dialogue answer is
marked as right or wrong (docs/DESIGN.md, dialogue rules).

## Structure decisions (owner, 13-10-2026)

- **Overcity is Rabenfels**, the city above. The naming detail does not matter much.
- **District and place names stay German on purpose**, also in the English base game
  ("Frieren" vibes): Die Ewige Halle, Der Schwarze Markt, Die Pilzgärten, Die Alte Kathedrale,
  Die Wächterfestung, Das Archiv, Die Verlorenen Bezirke, Das Herzgewölbe. The English names in
  the table above are only explanations.
- **The Overcity is the main area.** The descent comes only after a long time of play, when the
  player feels the city is all there is. Rumours of a city below exist, but the player should
  slowly have stopped believing in them.
- **End of act 1:** after the fight against the act-1 end boss, the hero is **teleported into
  the Underground City**. The descent is involuntary.
- **The act-1 end boss cannot be killed in act 1**, like Ketheric Thorm in BG3: he is
  invulnerable until the player later finds out how to take that protection away.
- **The cultists** (the existing crypt level with the Cult Priest) are a **small side story**
  that has nothing to do with the Underground City.
- **All 3 companions are found only in the Overcity.** The knight boy from the Underground
  City joins as a **short-term 4th companion**.
- **Act 1 will be long** (a lot of Overcity before the descent), but it is built in small
  slices; the whole story does not need to be in the game yet.
